Commercial transparency

Retail link status.

ProGameFinds currently does not publish Amazon Special Links, other affiliate links or retailer product-shopping links.

Current status

All Amazon product-shopping links have been removed from the catalogue. No Associate Tracking ID is being used in the site's product links, and ProGameFinds is not currently claiming or reporting affiliate commissions from visitors' purchases.

Product research only

Product cards are editorial research candidates, not verified retailer listings or offers. Exact ASINs, sellers, variants, prices, stock, delivery options and regional availability are not represented as checked. Visitors should independently verify the exact product and seller before purchasing from any retailer.

Future changes

Any future affiliate or retailer-shopping links will be added only after the appropriate account holder has confirmed programme eligibility and the website's disclosure and link-label requirements are in place.
