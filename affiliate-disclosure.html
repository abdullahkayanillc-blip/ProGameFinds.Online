Affiliate disclosure

Affiliate Disclosure

Clear disclosure helps visitors understand when a link may generate a commission.

ProGameFinds editorial information · Updated November 19, 2026

Application-status control: The current site project carries a pending-application notice. The owner must check Associates Central before publication and must not describe ProGameFinds as approved until Amazon confirms acceptance.

Amazon Associates disclosure

ProGameFinds is being prepared for participation in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. The required program statement is: “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.” This statement is displayed on the site as a compliance control; it is not proof that Amazon has approved the account.

How affiliate links work

Some links on this site may contain an affiliate tracking identifier. When a visitor follows an eligible Special Link and completes a qualifying purchase under the program terms, the site may earn a commission. The retailer controls checkout, fulfilment, returns, prices and availability. ProGameFinds does not take payment for retailer orders.

Link-level disclosures such as “(paid link)” are placed next to affiliate calls to action so visitors can identify them before clicking.

Product information

Product details, seller identity, pricing, stock, shipping, warranty and availability can change. Check the current retailer listing and manufacturer source before buying. ProGameFinds does not display Amazon customer reviews, star ratings, prices or stock feeds in this version of the site.

Editorial independence

Affiliate relationships do not constitute a guarantee, sponsorship or endorsement by a manufacturer. The site aims to explain general selection criteria and clearly identify content that is not hands-on testing.

Questions

For questions about a link or disclosure, use the contact section on the home page. Contact submissions are not enabled until the site owner configures and tests a delivery destination.
