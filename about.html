About the publication

Independent equipment research, clearly explained.

ProGameFinds is an editorial information project focused on fitness and home-gym equipment selection.

What we do

We explain how to compare equipment categories, read manufacturer documentation, assess space and installation requirements, and check retailer listing details. We aim to separate supported facts from unknowns.

What we do not do

ProGameFinds does not manufacture, stock, sell, install or fulfil equipment. We do not guarantee product performance, fitness outcomes, prices, availability or seller conduct. We do not provide medical advice or individual training plans.

Commercial transparency

As of 25 November 2026, the website has no active Amazon Special Links and does not claim to be an approved Amazon Associate. Any future Amazon-only affiliate feature will remain disabled until a legally eligible account holder independently confirms program approval and the assigned account details.

Editorial corrections

We welcome corrections to product facts, model numbers, page content or source references. Contact support@progamefinds.online and include the page URL and the evidence that should be reviewed.
