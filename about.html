About ProGameFinds

About ProGameFinds

ProGameFinds helps players make more informed gaming-setup decisions through practical buying guides, category organization and product-listing checks.

ProGameFinds editorial information · Updated November 19, 2026

Our purpose

A better gaming setup is not simply a list of expensive products. The right choices depend on fit, platform compatibility, space, comfort, the games you play and the budget you set. ProGameFinds organizes those questions so shoppers can compare the details that matter to them.

What you will find

  • Guides for mice, keyboards, headsets and controllers.
  • Planning advice for desks, chairs, lighting and complete setups.
  • Checklists for confirming model numbers, compatibility, seller information, return terms and warranty conditions.

Our editorial approach

We aim to explain buying criteria in plain language and distinguish general guidance from product-specific facts. Unless a page expressly says otherwise and documents its method, our guides are not hands-on laboratory tests, personal endorsements or claims of measured performance. We do not invent review scores, customer ratings, prices, stock status or test results.

Product names and listing identifiers shown on the site are candidate listings supplied in the site project. Their exact identifier-to-model matches, seller, regional version and current availability must be checked against the live retailer listing and manufacturer information before a purchase.

Affiliate transparency

Some outbound links may be Special Links to Amazon. A qualifying purchase may result in a commission where the site is eligible and the relevant program terms are met. Affiliate relationships do not change the price charged by the retailer. See our affiliate disclosure.

What ProGameFinds is not

ProGameFinds is not a retailer, does not process orders or payments, and does not provide manufacturer warranties. Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. ProGameFinds is not affiliated with or endorsed by the brands listed.
