About ProGameFinds

ProGameFinds is an independent gaming-gear discovery and education site for PC and console players. Our aim is to make product research easier by explaining the questions buyers should ask before choosing mice, headsets, controllers, and setup accessories.

How we work

  • We prioritise practical buying criteria, platform compatibility, comfort, and ownership considerations.
  • We distinguish editorial guidance from hands-on testing. We do not claim lab measurements or personal testing unless an article clearly documents it.
  • We avoid fabricated star ratings, sales rankings, prices, stock claims, and performance benchmarks.
  • We direct readers to manufacturer and retailer pages to confirm the latest model details and terms.

How the site earns

Some links are affiliate links. If you buy through them, ProGameFinds may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate relationships do not change the need for accurate, useful editorial information.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Corrections and contact

If you notice an outdated model detail, broken link, or editorial error, use the contact form on our home page.
