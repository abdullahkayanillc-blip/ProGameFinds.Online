Editorial standards

Our verification method.

We never treat a product name, source link or listing image as complete verification.

1. Identify the exact item

Record manufacturer, model number, revision, size and selected variant. Category-level ideas are not presented as verified products.

2. Check primary documentation

Use official manuals, specification sheets and manufacturer support pages to establish model features. Record the page checked and any detail it does not establish.

3. Verify marketplace facts separately

Retailer model match, live listing, seller, fulfilment, bundle, size, colour and regional version are separate checks. A manufacturer page does not prove a marketplace listing matches it.

4. Record the status precisely

Source recorded means a primary source page is identified; model verified means official evidence establishes the exact model; retailer mapping verified means the marketplace item matches the exact model and variant; seller checked means the current seller and fulfilment details are recorded; not checked means no reliable evidence has been captured.

5. Keep commerce disabled until eligible

No distributor or manufacturer shopping links are used. Amazon-only affiliate links are disabled and must not be activated by anyone who is not legally eligible to accept the Associates agreement. Account approval and assigned link IDs must be independently confirmed by the actual account holder.

6. No stale price or review claims

We do not scrape or publish unverified prices, stock, customer reviews or ratings. Product claims must be supported by current, permitted evidence and clearly dated.
