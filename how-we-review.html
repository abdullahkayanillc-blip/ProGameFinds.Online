Editorial methodology

How we review gear.

Our process separates a product idea from a verified retailer listing. A model name alone is never enough.

01Identify exact model, generation and SKUIdentity
02Check platform, operating system and connection compatibilityCompatibility
03Compare official manufacturer documentation and manualsEvidence
04Inspect retailer listing, seller, fulfilment, condition and included itemsListing
05Publish price or stock only with a current, time-stamped sourceFreshness
