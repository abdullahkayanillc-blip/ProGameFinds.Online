Research methodology

How our research works.

We separate a product idea, a manufacturer-confirmed model and a verified retailer listing. Each is a different evidence state.

01Identify the exact manufacturer, model, generation, revision, SKU and region.Identity
02Use official product pages, manuals and support documents for manufacturer specifications.Primary source
03Check platform, operating system, connection type, firmware needs and accessory compatibility.Compatibility
04Keep retailer ASIN, seller, condition, variant, fulfilment and live listing checks separate from manufacturer facts.Listing
05Publish volatile price or availability details only when a current, dated and permitted source is available.Freshness
06Label original artwork as illustration and record usage rights for any third-party media.Media rights

Evidence labels

  • Research candidate: a product family or model idea that still needs exact verification.
  • Manufacturer source recorded: an official manufacturer reference exists; this does not verify a retailer listing.
  • Compatibility checked: the exact model and platform combination have supporting documentation.
  • Retailer listing verified: the exact listing, seller, variant and region have been checked independently and recorded with a date.
  • Hands-on tested: used only when a documented physical test has actually been completed. This label is not inferred from research.

Current archive limitations

The historical archive retains 22 research candidates, including 20 historical ASIN references. Its published evidence snapshot records zero verified ASIN-to-model mappings and zero completed retailer listing checks. Manufacturer references do not change that status.

We do not publish unverified prices, stock, ratings, review counts, seller claims or commission estimates.

Corrections

Send a correction to support@progamefinds.online. Include the page URL, exact product model and a reliable source so the editorial record can be reviewed.
