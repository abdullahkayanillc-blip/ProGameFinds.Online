Buying guide

A practical equipment safety checklist

A pre-purchase and setup checklist focused on documentation, stability and maintenance.

Before purchase

Confirm the exact model, intended use, age or user restrictions, dimensions, capacity statements, installation conditions and warranty information.

Before assembly

Read the complete manual, check package contents and inspect components for damage. Use the recommended assembly method and seek qualified help for heavy or fixed installations.

Before use

Follow the manufacturer’s inspection guidance. Do not use equipment with damaged cables, unstable frames, loose fasteners or missing guards.

Keep the area clear

Keep exits, walkways and access to electrical switches clear. Store accessories so they cannot fall or become trip hazards.

Use suitable supervision

Children and teens should have appropriate adult supervision and qualified guidance around gym equipment. Stop if instructions, fit or safe operation are unclear.

Verification and safety: This guide is editorial information, not medical advice or an exercise prescription. Verify the exact product model, variant, seller and installation requirements. Children and teens should have appropriate adult supervision and qualified guidance around gym equipment.
