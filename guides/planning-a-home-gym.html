Buying guide

Planning a home gym around your space

A measurement-first checklist for fitting equipment into a shared or dedicated room.

Start with the room, not the equipment

Measure the usable floor area, ceiling height, doorways, stairs and the route used to move equipment into the room. Include space needed to open doors, adjust equipment and keep walkways clear.

Check the building and floor

Confirm the floor type and any building or tenancy restrictions before choosing heavy equipment. Do not assume a mat makes every floor suitable for a concentrated load. Ask a qualified installer or building professional about anchoring or load questions.

Plan storage and shared use

Keep accessories in stable, purpose-built storage. Avoid placing equipment where it blocks exits, windows, heaters or everyday circulation routes. Consider how other household members use the room.

Review delivery and assembly

Check package dimensions, weight, access requirements, assembly instructions, tools and the manufacturer’s recommended installation conditions before ordering.

Make the final checklist

Record measurements, electrical needs, noise considerations, service access, warranty conditions and the exact model/variant you intend to compare.
