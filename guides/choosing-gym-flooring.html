Buying guide

Choosing gym flooring and mats

Questions to ask about floor protection, surface compatibility, cleaning and storage.

Identify the underlying surface

Tile, timber, concrete and carpet behave differently. Confirm whether the proposed flooring is suitable for the existing surface and whether moisture or trapped debris could cause damage.

Check material and thickness claims

Compare the manufacturer’s stated material, dimensions, thickness and intended application. Do not assume thicker automatically means suitable for every machine or load.

Assess slip, edge and trip risks

Review how pieces join, whether edges can curl, and whether the final installation leaves a raised transition or blocked threshold.

Plan cleaning and ventilation

Read care instructions and consider odour, moisture, ventilation and cleaning products compatible with the material.

Store unused pieces safely

Keep stacked mats and accessories stable and away from walkways, heat sources and exits.

Verification and safety: This guide is editorial information, not medical advice or an exercise prescription. Verify the exact product model, variant, seller and installation requirements. Children and teens should have appropriate adult supervision and qualified guidance around gym equipment.
