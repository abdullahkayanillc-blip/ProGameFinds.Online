Buying guide

Comparing cardio equipment

A neutral comparison framework for treadmills, exercise bikes, ellipticals and rowers.

Measure the footprint

Compare the full operating footprint, not only folded or stored dimensions. Check ceiling clearance, access around moving parts and the space needed for safe entry and exit.

Review controls and safety features

Read the exact model’s manual for stop controls, emergency-stop arrangements, speed or resistance controls and user-limit requirements. Never rely only on a product photo.

Consider noise and installation

Check power requirements, floor suitability, vibration, noise and any special delivery or assembly requirements. Ask the seller or manufacturer about service availability in your region.

Compare maintenance needs

Look for the manufacturer’s cleaning and inspection schedule, consumables, replacement parts and warranty exclusions.

Verify the exact listing

Match the model number, variant, included accessories and regional specification against manufacturer documentation and the retailer listing.

Verification and safety: This guide is editorial information, not medical advice or an exercise prescription. Verify the exact product model, variant, seller and installation requirements. Children and teens should have appropriate adult supervision and qualified guidance around gym equipment.
