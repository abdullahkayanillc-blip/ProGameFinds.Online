Controller Platform Compatibility Checklist

Check console generation, PC support, connection modes, button mapping and included accessories before choosing a gaming controller.

Identify the exact controller model

Controller product families can include wired, wireless, licensed, platform-specific and revised editions with similar names. Record the full model name, model number and hardware revision from manufacturer documentation. A family name alone does not establish compatibility.

Match the platform and connection

Check each intended device separately: console generation, computer operating system, handheld device and mobile operating system. Then confirm the connection supported on each platform. A controller may use a cable on one console, a receiver on a PC and Bluetooth on a mobile device—or may not support all three.

Check whether the receiver is included and paired, and whether a firmware update or supported driver is required. Do not assume every USB cable carries data; some are charge-only.

Check controls and special features

Verify the number and location of extra buttons, paddles, trigger stops, audio jacks, motion controls and profile switches. Some features may only work on one platform or connection mode. Confirm how mapping is saved and whether an app must remain installed.

Power and accessories

Wired controllers may draw power from the connected device. Wireless models may use replaceable or rechargeable batteries. Check charging method, included accessories, cable length, receiver storage and whether a charging dock is sold separately or included in the exact edition.

Pre-purchase checklist

  • Write down the exact model number and hardware revision.
  • Verify each target platform and its supported connection mode.
  • Confirm receiver, cable, battery and charging-dock inclusion.
  • Check extra buttons, audio and motion features on your platform.
  • Review firmware support, warranty and return terms.
