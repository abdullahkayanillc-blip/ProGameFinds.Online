Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current static website and its planned contact capability. It must be updated if new analytics, advertising, form or account services are enabled.

ProGameFinds editorial information · Updated November 19, 2026

Current website state

ProGameFinds is a static informational website. This version does not provide visitor accounts, checkout, user profiles, advertising pixels or first-party analytics. It does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies.

Hosting and technical requests

Pages are delivered through Cloudflare's website infrastructure. To deliver and secure a web request, hosting and network providers may process technical data such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamps and security events under their own service terms and privacy notices.

Contact form

The contact form displayed in this version is disabled. Its fields cannot be submitted and no contact message is transmitted by this page. The form must not be enabled until the owner has selected a delivery provider, confirmed the destination inbox, published accurate provider disclosures and tested successful delivery and failure handling.

When a contact service is activated in a future release, this policy must identify the provider, data fields, purpose, retention and any relevant transfer or subprocessors before collecting submissions.

Affiliate and external links

Links may take you to Amazon or another third-party website. When you leave ProGameFinds, that provider's privacy policy, cookies, tracking and terms apply. Some Amazon links may include an affiliate tracking identifier. See our affiliate disclosure.

Children and sensitive information

This site is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children under 13. Do not send payment credentials, passwords, identity documents or other sensitive information through public website forms or messages.

Policy changes and contact

This policy must be reviewed whenever the website adds analytics, advertising, forms, chat, accounts or other third-party services. Questions can be directed through the home page contact section once the owner activates a verified contact destination.
