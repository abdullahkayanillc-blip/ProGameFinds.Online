Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This page describes the information that may be processed when you use ProGameFinds. This is a general website notice and should be reviewed against the services actually enabled on the live site.

If you use the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, topic, and message. The current website code contains an unconfigured contact-form placeholder. Do not assume messages are delivered until the site owner configures and tests the form provider and destination inbox.

Third-party services

The website may load font files from Google Fonts. When you click an affiliate link, your browser leaves ProGameFinds and visits Amazon; Amazon may process information under its own privacy notice, cookies, and terms. We do not control third-party services.

Cookies and analytics

The current static page does not intentionally set first-party analytics cookies. This statement must be revisited before enabling analytics, advertising pixels, embedded video, or other tracking services.

Data retention and requests

We do not promise that a contact message is stored or delivered while the form remains unconfigured. For privacy questions or correction requests, use the contact details/form on the home page once active.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when site features or applicable requirements change.
