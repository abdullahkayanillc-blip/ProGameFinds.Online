Privacy information

Privacy policy.

This page describes the limited information currently collected through the ProGameFinds website.

Website operation

ProGameFinds is hosted as a static website on Cloudflare Pages. Hosting infrastructure may process technical connection information required to deliver and protect the website. We do not currently operate an account system, checkout, customer profile database or on-site product-order process.

Search and preferences

The equipment-category search and filter controls run in your browser and do not submit search terms to a ProGameFinds application backend.

Contact email

If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose to include in that message and use it to respond to your enquiry. Do not include sensitive personal or financial information.

Cookies and advertising

ProGameFinds does not currently set analytics or affiliate advertising cookies through its own site code. This may change only after a documented review and an updated privacy notice. External websites you choose to visit operate under their own privacy terms.

Children and teens

The website is general equipment-selection information, not a service designed to collect personal information from children. Contact us through an adult where appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@progamefinds.online.
