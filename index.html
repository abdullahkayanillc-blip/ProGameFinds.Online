How we research

Evidence first. No guessed specifications.

We distinguish official manufacturer information from retailer listing facts, seller identity and variant data. A source link alone does not prove that a marketplace listing matches the exact model.

01 · Identify

Record the exact product name, model number and variant.

02 · Verify

Compare model facts against current official documentation.

03 · Review

Check retailer page, seller and fulfilment details separately.

04 · Disclose

Keep commercial relationships clear and never claim unapproved affiliation.

Responsible equipment research

Good information should make decisions clearer.

Equipment is not a shortcut to health or a guaranteed result. Product choice should account for the intended user, instructions, available space and safe installation. Children and teens should have appropriate adult supervision and qualified guidance around gym equipment.
