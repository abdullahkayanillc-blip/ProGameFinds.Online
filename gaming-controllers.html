Gaming Controllers

Controller compatibility can be more complicated than a product title suggests. Confirm the exact console, PC or mobile platform and connection method before choosing.

Category buying hub · Updated November 23, 2026 · General guidance, not hands-on product testing.

Confirm platform support first

Check the manufacturer's compatibility list for the exact console generation, operating system and game environment. Similar-looking controllers can support different platforms.

Compare connection modes

Wired USB, Bluetooth and proprietary wireless connections are not interchangeable. Check whether a cable or receiver is included and whether wireless features work on your intended device.

Review controls and ergonomics

Compare stick placement, trigger shape, button layout, grip texture and controller dimensions. Additional buttons or adjustable triggers are useful only if they suit your games and preferences.

Check revisions and software

A product family may contain multiple revisions or licensed editions. Verify the model code, firmware support, configuration app and included accessories.

Before you buy

  • Confirm exact console/PC/mobile compatibility.
  • Verify wired/wireless modes and included receiver or cable.
  • Check model code, edition and revision.
  • Review button layout and physical dimensions.
  • Confirm firmware/software support and seller details.

Product listings, prices, stock, sellers, revisions and warranty terms can change. Check the exact model with the manufacturer and current retailer listing before ordering.
