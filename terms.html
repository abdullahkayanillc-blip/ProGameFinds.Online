Website terms

Terms of use.

These terms explain the limits of ProGameFinds as an independent editorial information website.

Informational purpose

ProGameFinds publishes general gaming gear research, product-category information, compatibility guidance and buying checklists. Content is provided for general information and is not a guarantee of product suitability, performance or availability.

Product facts and verification

Specifications, model revisions, platform support, firmware, seller details, warranty conditions and availability can change. A product candidate or historical ASIN reference is not a verified retailer listing. Check the exact model and current manufacturer documentation before relying on a product detail.

Testing and editorial claims

We do not represent research summaries as hands-on tests. Any future hands-on testing claim must be supported by a documented test method and the relevant test conditions. Illustrative artwork is not product photography.

Retailer and affiliate links

ProGameFinds currently publishes no retailer product-shopping links, Amazon Special Links or affiliate tracking links. We do not claim commission earnings from visitor purchases. The current position is described on the retail link status page.

External services

Some pages may identify manufacturers or products by name for editorial context. ProGameFinds does not control third-party websites, their content, privacy practices, terms, pricing or fulfilment. Do not assume an external service has been independently reviewed merely because it is mentioned.

Intellectual property and corrections

Product and company names belong to their respective owners and are used only to identify products or explain compatibility. For corrections or questions, contact support@progamefinds.online.
