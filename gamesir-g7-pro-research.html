Before you choose

Strengths and checks.

Features that stand out

  • TMR sticks and Hall Effect analog triggers are the headline hardware features.
  • One controller can cover wired Xbox play, 2.4GHz/wired PC play and Bluetooth Android use.
  • Four mappable controls and PC-only gyro provide additional customization options.
  • The charging station and rechargeable battery are included in the standard G7 Pro package according to GameSir documentation.

Checks before purchase

  • Do not assume wireless Xbox support: GameSir specifies a wired Xbox connection.
  • The gyroscope mapping feature is PC-only.
  • Confirm the exact colour/edition, seller, warranty, return terms and included accessories for your country.
  • Button feel, wireless reliability and long-term durability are personal-use questions; this profile does not claim hands-on testing.
