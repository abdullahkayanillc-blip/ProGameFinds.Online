# ProGameFinds product and media audit — 20 November 2026

## Scope and interpretation

This is a source-backed cross-check of the 22 catalogue cards on the isolated preview branch. It is **not** a live Amazon.com account-session audit. A product-to-ASIN mapping may be corroborated by a manufacturer page, an Amazon regional listing or independent catalogue records, but that does not prove the current Amazon.com offer is live, the seller is authorised, the exact colour/package is selected, or the item ships to a buyer's address.

Audit terms:
- **Cross-reference found**: independent source(s) associate the model with the ASIN.
- **Manufacturer page checked**: the manufacturer confirms the product family/specifications.
- **Amazon.com offer / seller / exact variant not checked**: these remain owner-side checks in the live Amazon listing.
- **Illustration only**: the existing SVG is original category artwork, not a photograph of the actual product and not UGC.
