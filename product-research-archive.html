Verification notice

These are historical research candidates, not confirmed Amazon listings. The 20 ASINs are recorded as unverified identifiers. There are no Amazon Special Links, affiliate tracking tags, retailer purchase links, current prices, ratings, reviews, sales estimates or stock claims on this page.

Evidence standard

Three separate checks

A manufacturer page can confirm a model’s published features. It does not prove that a particular marketplace ASIN is that model. Seller, edition, region and live offer require their own independent checks.

01 · ManufacturerModel-family specifications and official support references.
02 · Marketplace identityASIN-to-model, variant, seller and region — not verified in this archive.
03 · Offer statusPrice, availability, ratings, reviews and delivery — not checked or published.
